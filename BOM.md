## Engineering items to resolve before claiming the design is complete

1. **Power:** A 5S LiPo battery is nominally 18.5 V and reaches 21 V when fully charged. The motherboard uses a 19 V input; document a suitable regulated supply stage and protections. Specify separate rails for the CM5 modules, displays, fans and pump, with current ratings and a power budget.
2. **Compute Modules:** Two CM5 modules need compatible carrier boards or a documented custom carrier PCB. Add the exact carrier board(s), connectors, schematics and PCB source files where applicable.
3. **Cooling:** Confirm the full thermal path, including cold plate, fittings, heat exchanger/radiator, pump operating point, coolant and leak-safe routing.
4. **Cameras and displays:** Verify exact board revisions, FPC connector/pinout, driver support, controller boards and the number of channels supported by the chosen carrier.
5. **Optics and mechanics:** Verify lens focal length and optical type, mounting tolerances, head fit, cable strain relief and fastening hardware against the CAD.
6. **Fabrication:** Add supplier quotes and specifications for each 3D-printed and CNC part.
